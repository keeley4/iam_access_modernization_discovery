01 · Why we are in the room

What these meetings decide.

What is being decided

Which system owns each fact: identity, allocation, entitlement. No product, cost or date is decided here.

What stays the same

XRAS and AMIE intake. Award decisions. Per-resource provisioning behaviour. Anything that only reads LDAP.

Out of scope here

Implementation, phasing, API specs, adoption tiers, cost, staffing.

What deferring costs

An undecided fact keeps today’s answer. Today’s answer is the fragmented one.

01
Which identity domains are in scope.
Six reach resources today: NCSA Kerberos, NetID, campus AD, ACCESS ID, Globus, Google. ICCP runs on NetID, outside acctd.
02
Amending IAM Policy v1.3.
NCSA identities only by default; CISO approval for linked identities; IIB approval for new IdM interfaces. Federation needs it amended.
03
Authority to close access-creating tools.
Thirteen tools can create or change access. Closing them needs owners and dates.
04
XRAS continuity past 30 April 2027.
The award funding XRAS ends then, no announced successor. If intake moves, that decision precedes the portal.
