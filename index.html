03 · The mechanism

acctd is the only path from award to running job.

How it is built

Service on idds-prod hands out work. acct.pl on each resource polls over SSH. Plugins execute locally, and all resource-specific behavior lives there.

What it does

Six functions: activate and inactivate, at project, account and user level. A charge group is the unit of authorization. No second grant path.

What it cannot do

Refunds cannot execute in Slurm, so a human adds SUs back. Partial deactivation has no interface: a stored procedure by hand, per tuple.
